# Tally Shop — Generic Customer Help (Sandbox)

**Audience:** Customers. **Source type:** Approved general guidance, not live commerce records.

- Available products, quantities and prices are maintained in the Tally catalogue tool. Never use this document as a stock or price list.
- Customer orders start awaiting payment. A transfer claim, screenshot or bank alert is not verified payment. A matching signed provider event is required to confirm an order.
- The customer may ask for their own current order status. A confirmed order may later become preparing, dispatched and fulfilled.
- A customer can ask Tally for merchant help with an unclear question, complaint or delivery exception. Tally records a case and seeks an authorized merchant response; it should try direct human handoff where supported. Never invent a refund policy or a guaranteed deadline.
- Customers may ask Tally for their case update in the same chat. Delivery of a merchant response is confirmed only by the connected channel, not by saving the case.
- All names, transactions and numbers used in this assessment are synthetic test data.
